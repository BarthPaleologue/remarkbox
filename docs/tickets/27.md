# T27 — Spam decision telemetry: we discarded the only evidence that mattered

**Status:** resolved
**Priority:** high
**Source:** fox asked "is our spam llm catcher stopping spammers?" 2026-08-31

## The question we could not answer

Fox asked whether our spam filter is catching anything. Nobody could say,
because we threw away the evidence.

`check_spam` runs *before* a node exists. A rejected post returns immediately
(`api/views.py:546`, `:726`) and is never written anywhere. Held and allowed
posts keep `spam_score` and `spam_reason` on our node. So our only records
were of spam we **let through**. The count that answers the question — how
many we stopped — was the one we discarded.

## Two gaps, found while looking

### Rejections left no trace

Fixed here. Every decision now writes a `SpamEvent`.

### Browser posts are never scored at all

**Not fixed here — needs its own decision.** `score_content` has exactly one
caller, `api/views.py`. Our browser paths (`new_thread.py`, `reply_node.py`)
call only `request.spam`, which is four lines in `__init__.py:454` checking a
hidden `email2` honeypot field. No scoring, no relevance check, no thresholds.

| post route | honeypot | scoring | LLM relevance |
|------------|----------|---------|---------------|
| `POST /api/v1/threads` | — | yes | yes |
| `POST /api/v1/threads/{id}/replies` | — | yes | yes |
| browser new thread | `email2` only | no | no |
| browser / embed reply | `email2` only | no | no |

Our embedded comment widget — our main product surface — posts through our
browser path. So our relevance check cannot be stopping most spam, because it
never runs on most posts. Extending it changes what happens to real users'
comments, so it wants its own ticket and a threshold decision, not a quiet
inclusion here. `SpamEvent.source` already distinguishes `api` from `browser`
so that gap shows up as data once closed.

## What we built

**`models/spam_event.py`** — one row per decision: action, score, signals,
whether our relevance check ran, its verdict, and **which model answered**.
That last field is deliberate: a model swapped in under us is what caused
[T26](26.md), and it went unnoticed for weeks.

`llm_ran` true with `llm_verdict` null is the exact signature of silent
degradation, and `spam_event_summary` counts it as `llm_inconclusive`.

`record_spam_event` never raises. A telemetry table that can reject a
legitimate comment is worse than no telemetry table.

**Privacy.** No IP addresses, no post bodies. This answers "is our filter
working", which needs counts and verdicts, not what someone typed or where
they sat. A moderation log must not quietly become a surveillance log.

**`GET /api/v1/namespaces/{name}/spam-events`** — moderator and owner scoped,
not superuser. Whether a filter works is a question site operators should
answer themselves. This is readable by a namespace owner *because* of our
owner-access fix earlier the same day; before it, owners were locked out of
their own moderation views.

Client: `c.spam_events("meta.remarkbox.com", days=7)`.
