# T20 — Full content privacy for namespaces

**Status:** open
**Priority:** medium
**Source:** follow-up from our 2026-07-27 security audit, raised while
implementing our private thread list

## Summary

`Namespace.public` now works, but it governs our **index only**: our home page
listing, thread-list API, search, RSS, sitemap, and whole-namespace export.
A thread reached by direct link, and every embedded comment widget, stays
readable on a private namespace.

That boundary is deliberate. Our embed product resolves a thread by its page
URI, so hiding individual threads would break embedded comments — which is
most of what Remarkbox does today. Index privacy is the piece that could ship
without breaking anyone.

## What is not covered

An operator who wants a genuinely private community — content unreadable to
non-members, not merely unlisted — does not get that from `public = False`.
Anyone holding or guessing a node id can still read a thread through
`/api/v1/threads/{id}`, our web permalink, or a per-thread export.

## Why this matters for undigg.com

T17's community-creation flow should not offer "private community" until this
exists. Index privacy is honest as "unlisted"; it is not honest as "private".
Label our current toggle accordingly in any signup UX.

## Design questions to settle first

- **Membership model.** Private content needs a member concept beyond our
  current owner/moderator roles. Does joining require an invite, an owner's
  approval, or an allowlisted email domain?
- **Embeds.** A private namespace that still embeds on a public page is a
  contradiction. Do we refuse embed mode for private namespaces, or serve a
  log-in prompt in the iframe?
- **Existing links.** Flipping a populated namespace to private would break
  every shared permalink. Warn in our settings UI before applying.
- **Search and notifications.** Mention notifications, watch emails, and
  webmentions all quote content; each needs a membership check.
